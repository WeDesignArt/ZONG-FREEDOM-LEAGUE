FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

1. How do I join Z-Hunt?
Open your My Zong App, click “Join the League”, and enter your details to get started. Complete your missions and submit your evidence through the same form.
2. Do I need to buy a handset to participate?
No. Z-Hunt is free to participate in. You can earn Freedom Points by completing different missions. A handset purchase is not mandatory for participation; however, it will help you climb the league faster.
3. How do I earn Freedom Points?
Complete the available Z-Hunt missions and submit the required evidence. Once your submission is verified, the corresponding Freedom Points will be added to your score.
4. Are rewards guaranteed if I complete all the missions?
Not necessarily. Completing missions and earning Freedom Points brings you closer to the rewards, but winners will be determined based on the final internal leaderboard. The highest-ranked participant in each league will win the respective league reward.
5. How will I know my Freedom Points?
Your points and leaderboard position will be tracked internally by Zong. Your submitted mission evidence will be reviewed and points will be allocated accordingly.
6. Can I complete the same mission more than once?
No. Each mission can only be completed once and points will only be awarded once per mission.
7. What counts as valid evidence for a mission?
Each mission will specify the required evidence. Make sure your photo, video, screenshot or other proof clearly demonstrates that you have completed the mission.
8. What hashtag should I use for social media missions?
For missions requiring a public social media post, use #ZHunt26 and make sure your post or story is visible for verification. Additionally, attach a screenshot of the post in the submission form.
9. What happens if my evidence is not accepted?
Only valid and verifiable evidence will qualify for Freedom Points. If the submitted evidence does not meet the mission requirements, points may not be awarded.
10. How do I move from Bronze to Silver or Gold?
The more missions you complete, the more Freedom Points you can earn. Your accumulated points determine your league position, allowing you to progress from Bronze to Silver to Gold.
11. What can I win?
Each league has its own exciting rewards. The top participant on the final internal leaderboard in each league will win the corresponding league reward.
12. When will the winners be announced?
The winner announcement date will be shared at a later stage. Keep an eye on the My Zong App and official Zong channels for updates.
13. Is there a cost to participate?
No. Z-Hunt is completely free to participate in. You only incur costs if you independently choose to purchase a handset, or avail any paid service.
14. Do I have to complete all the missions?
No, you can participate by completing the missions you choose. However, every completed mission earns you points and can help improve your position on the leaderboard.
15. Can I submit evidence after completing my missions?
Yes. You can complete your missions and collect your evidence, then submit the required evidence for all completed missions through the “Join the League” form.
16. What if two participants have the same number of points?
In case of a tie, the final ranking will be determined based on the Zong internal leaderboard and applicable campaign criteria.
